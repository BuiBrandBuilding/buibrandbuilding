[ 07 ] Questions

Things people usually ask.

How long does a typical project take?

Most brand or website projects launch in 2–4 weeks from kickoff. Larger or more complex builds can take 4–6 weeks. You'll get a clear timeline with milestones before we ever start.

Do I need to have a brand already, or can you build one from scratch?

Either works. We can take your existing brand and elevate it, or build a full identity from scratch — logo, colors, typography, guidelines, the whole system. Many clients choose the bundled brand + web package.

What platforms do you build on?

We match the tools to your project — whatever gives you the best balance of speed, ease of updating, and long-term flexibility. The goal is always the same: fast, clean, easy for you to manage, and built to grow with your business.

Do you write copy, or do I need to provide it?

Copywriting is an optional add-on. Many clients bring their own copy and we guide the structure; others hire us to handle the entire messaging and copy end-to-end for the site.

What happens after launch?

You get two free weeks of post-launch tweaks, full ownership of your files, and the option to stay on a monthly care plan if you'd like us to keep the site running smoothly and updated.

How much is the typical total investment?

Websites start at $800 and brand identity projects start at $400. Most full projects (brand + site + launch) land somewhere between $1,000 and $2,500 depending on scope. You'll always get a fixed quote — no surprises.

Do you work with businesses outside your area?

Yes — we work remotely with clients across the US and internationally. Everything happens over video calls, shared docs, and async updates, so location never gets in the way.
